# Vince services, packages and training

The commercial catalogue: how Vince sells consulting time and training, and
what each package actually commits to.

## Provenance and the one rule for this topic

Compiled from Vince's Notion workspace on **2026-09-22**.

**Never quote a price from memory.** The figures move, they exist in four
currencies, and at least one training package's price disagrees between two
live pages (see *Known contradictions*). Always open the
[Professional Services Price List](https://app.notion.com/p/b9165e6b585248948ca16549ae53cf05)
and read the current number. This skill carries **structure** — package names,
what is included, the terms and the boundaries — because that is what is stable
and what consultants actually get wrong.

## Vocabulary

**T&M** Time and Material · **CaaS** Consulting as a Service · **CaaS
Responsible** the customer's single point of contact · **Rollover Days** unused
days that survive into the next period · **Refresher** a short single-session
training · **Certified Vince Partner** a partner tier with a standing discount ·
**Queue Manager** allocates portal-ordered work · **Moment** the PSA tool
holding hours.

## 1. Professional Services (T&M)

Three role ladders, priced identically per seniority level:

- **Consulting** — Principal / Lead Consultant–Lead Solution Architect / Senior
  Consultant / Consultant / Associate Consultant / Junior Associate Consultant
- **Development** — Principal → Junior Associate Developer
- **Project Management** — Principal → Associate Project Coordinator

Four currency columns (EUR / USD / NOK, plus SEK for T&M).

**The terms that catch people out:**

| Term | Rule |
|---|---|
| Standard hours | Mon–Fri, 08–18 local time |
| Scheduled work outside standard hours | **+100%** |
| Travel and standby | billed at **50%** |
| Emergency (booked less than 48 h ahead) | **+100%** |
| Certified Vince Partner | **10% discount** |
| Default | billed T&M unless otherwise agreed |

## 2. CaaS — Consulting as a Service

Usable for assistance with any Vince **or Infor** product — Consulting,
Maintenance, General Care, Interface, App Building. A fixed contact manages
staffing. Prices are CPI-regulated annually.

**Monthly CaaS** — billed monthly in advance, **3-month termination notice**
(so a 3-month minimum), and **at most 50% of purchased days are guaranteed in
any one week**:

| Tier | Days/month | Rollover |
|---|---|---|
| Bronze | 1 | 0 |
| Silver | 2 | 0 |
| Gold | 3 | 1 |
| Platinum | 4 | 1 |

**Yearly CaaS** — billed annually in advance, **max 5 consulting days secured
per month** (bigger months are best-effort):

| Tier | Days/year | Rollover |
|---|---|---|
| Iron | 3 | 0 |
| Bronze | 10 | 5 |
| Silver | 15 | 5 |
| Gold | 20 | 5 |
| Platinum | 25 | 5 |
| Titanium | 30 | 5 |
| Tungsten | 35 | 5 |
| Vanadium | 40 | 5 |
| Niobium | 45 | 5 |
| Rhodium | 50 | 5 |

Larger packages on request.

**Operationally:** the *customer* registers CaaS tasks in the Customer Portal;
the *consultant* follows up. If the month's hours run out with tasks still
open, tell the CSM — see the `vince-customer-portal` skill.

## 3. Onboarding packages

Same prices across all Vince products (Vince Live, App Builder, Vince Security
and so on).

| Package | Duration | Extra beyond the common core |
|---|---|---|
| Fast Track | ~2 weeks | — |
| Standard | ~3 weeks | + 8 h |
| Advanced | ~4 weeks | + 24 h |
| Professional | ~4 weeks, T&M | "as needed" |

The common core in all four: Tech Setup, Pre-Defined Workflows, Instructional
Videos, and **2 training sessions** (Professional: 2+). The extra hours are
Hackathon / customer-specific assistance / advisory.

**Hard boundary:** onboarding must complete **within 2 months**. Anything after
that becomes a regular Change Order, billed at the Professional Services price
list. Say this early in an onboarding, not when the clock has run out.

## 4. Training & Continuous Enablement

Freestanding — orderable at any time, regardless of participant count. Custom
training on request.

**Named packages** (topic × level, session counts from the price list):

- Vince Live Workflows — Basic 2, Advanced 2, Refresher 1
- Vince Dashboards — Basic 3, Advanced 3
- Vince Butler duties and dashboards — Basic 2
- Vince Security — Advanced 2 *(the Training Packages page also lists Vince
  Security (SOD) Basic)*
- Vince Coding — Basic 3, Advanced 3, Expert 3
- Vince Data Lake and tables — Basic 1
- Vince Automation — Basic 1
- Vince Classic — Basic 2, Advanced 2
- Vince App-Builder — Basic 2, Inspiration Workshop 1, Hackathon = T&M
- Vince PEPPOL — Basic, Advanced *(on the Training Packages page only, not on
  the price list)*

**How a training engagement runs:**

- Sessions are **1.5 h**; the final session gets **+0.5 h** for a closing
  segment from the operations manager (feedback and further consultancy needs).
- The consultant is additionally available for questions **during and up to 2
  weeks after**, spending up to **2 hours**.
- Sold either by a sales rep (who books a consultant's calendar during or after
  the meeting) or **via the Customer Portal**, where it enters as a
  pre-approved Change Order and the Queue Manager allocates it.
- Target: both sessions within 2 weeks; **never more than 4 weeks apart**;
  **after 8 weeks, outstanding sessions are cancelled and must be re-ordered**.
- **Invoiced in full as soon as ordered.**
- Recording of sound or screen is permitted.

**Readiness — check before promising a date.** Each package row carries a status
and a named consultant roster. Several read *"Needs material"* or *"Needs
content and materials"*. As of this writing **only Vince Live Workflows Basic is
marked Ready.** Check the package's own status before selling or scheduling it.

## Known contradictions — do not treat any single source as authoritative

These are live disagreements in the documentation, not errors in this file:

- **Vince Dashboards Basic**: the price list says 3 sessions; the *Vince
  Dashboard – Basic* package page describes 3 lessons plus kick-off, QnA and
  closing (6 meetings) and quotes a **different price**.
- **Session count**: Training Packages' generic scope text says "each package
  will include 2 separate training sessions", which contradicts every 3-session
  row.
- **Catalogue mismatch**: Vince PEPPOL and Vince Security (SOD) Basic appear on
  Training Packages but have no matching price-list row.

When these matter, get a ruling rather than picking one.
